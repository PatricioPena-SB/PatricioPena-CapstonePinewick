# Engagement Kickoff: Pinewick Service & Returns

**Attendees:** Renee Castellanos (VP Customer Operations, sponsor), Gabe Lindqvist (Returns & Warranty Manager), Aisha Boateng (Branch Operations Director), Neil Prasad (IT Manager), Saltbox delivery team (PM, PC, TA)

**Purpose:** Introduce the team, confirm scope and objectives, agree the working cadence, and schedule Align workshops.

## What we heard

- Renee: the storefront project "changed how buyers feel about Pinewick." Returns are the number-one complaint in the buyer survey. The metric she cares about is support contacts about returns and warranty. She wants them halved.
- Gabe: there is a written return policy (30-day window, 15% restocking, exceptions) but "every branch has its own version." He wants one policy, enforced by the system, and he wants to stop being the person who arbitrates fee waivers by phone.
- Aisha: branches are worried about being handed returns they didn't sell. Drop-offs need to be visible before the buyer shows up with a pallet of fittings. Chemicals and aerosols can't be shipped back by buyers. It's a DOT issue, so they are drop-off only.
- Neil: the ERP vendor delivered an RMA API last year for a different project; it works but "isn't fast." Credit memos post when receiving posts, nightly. He has one Salesforce admin (himself) and a part-time developer.
- Warranty claims: mostly power tools and some pumps. Each manufacturer has its own form. Gabe's team fills a spreadsheet and emails it. The manufacturer replies in "days to weeks." Serial numbers are written on the spreadsheet by hand from a photo the buyer sends.

## Decisions

- Theme sequencing: Returns & Warranty Claims first. Order Support Cases and Account Self-Service follow and are not part of the first Prototype Review.
- Gabe is theme owner for Returns & Warranty Claims and will attend the workshop and the prototype walkthrough.
- Renee signs off per theme.
- Neil to provide read access to the ERP RMA API documentation and a sandbox endpoint before the workshop.

## Open questions carried into the workshop

1. What exactly is the return window, and from which date does it run?
2. Which categories are non-returnable, and is that list stable?
3. How are contractual restocking-fee exceptions recorded today?
4. What does the manufacturer need for a warranty claim, per manufacturer?
5. Partial returns: can a buyer return part of a line?
6. Who approves a return today, and does anyone need to approve one in the future state?

## Cadence

- Align workshops: Returns & Warranty Claims this week; the other two themes the following week.
- Weekly status to Renee; daily stand-up with the Saltbox team; Gabe available for questions asynchronously.
